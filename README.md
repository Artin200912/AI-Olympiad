# AI Olympiad Class Materials

Course materials organized by subject, week, and exam.

## Contents

- `subjects/calculus/` — calculus exercises.
- `subjects/probability/` — probability and combinatorics exercises.
- `subjects/linear-algebra/` — linear algebra class materials, including week 1 notes and sections 8–6.
- `subjects/python-coding/` — programming notebooks and their supporting data, including machine-learning coding sessions. Eight queens is in week 2, graph search is in week 5, and KNN is in week 16.
- `subjects/machine-learning/` — machine-learning notes and readings, including the 13 Mehr and 29 Aban notes.
- `subjects/exercise-solving/` — teacher-led exercise-solving materials.
- `exams/` — numbered exams, summer theory exams, and the marathon, with questions and solutions where available.
- `books/` — reference books and solutions.
- `additional-resources/summer-course-1405/` — summer-course calculus, linear algebra, and probability practice sheets.
- `additional-resources/probability/` — additional probability notes, including the Zarchi statistics notes.
